    (W) Take a real-world operational system that can be observed (bank, supermarket) and identify the elements associated with it in terms of variability, complexity, interconnectedness.

Grocery Store. 

- Variability, there are probably more customers at lunch time, afterwork, weekends. Probably should address this by redistributing the staff to certain stations, like the cashiers, etc. We cant know the exact number tho, or how many products each customer buys, but we can simulate a rough estimate from past data. Also cant predict if a staff member is sick, but we also know certain times of the year, people are more likely to get sick.

- Complexity. Cashiers, self-checkout, the cooked food, and fresh fish section at the back. Holidays can change work staff availability and customer traffic.

- Interconnectedness. Slow cashier or customer can cause a super long que to form, shelves arent restocked fast enough, or out of stock items cus supply chains are disrupted, moving staff around might cause one section of the store to not be maintained, or get overlooked, self-checkout with 1 supervisor could help alleviate from of this man power issues.
